1. Baseline 20-Stock Index Portfolio (Equal Weight: 5.0% Each)

The index maintains a baseline of 20 large-cap US blue-chip equities (5.0% equal weight each, totaling 100.0%). A secondary reserve pool (AMD, NFLX, WMT, BAC, CRM, ORCL, CSCO, QCOM, CVX, GS) is monitored to immediately replace constituents upon removal.

  • REMOVE: Constituent weight drops to 0.0% upon catastrophic negative event or severe credit/insolvency news.
  • ADD: Top-ranked candidate from reserve pool is promoted to 5.0% weight to restore 20-stock universe.
  • OVERWEIGHT: Constituent weight is boosted (up to 8.5%) on high-impact positive earnings or product innovations.
  • UNDERWEIGHT: Constituent weight is reduced (down to 1.5%) on negative news pressure.
  • HOLD: Baseline weight (5.0%) maintained when sentiment is neutral.
